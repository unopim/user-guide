# Admin Essentials

UnoPim 3.0 modernizes the admin panel you work in every day. This page covers the small things you will notice first — themes, navigation, saving, notifications — so nothing surprises you.

<ImagePopup src="/assets/3.0/images/introduction/admin-dark-theme.png" alt="The admin panel in dark theme" />

## Light and dark theme

The admin now ships with a dark theme alongside the light one.

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner.
2. Choose **Theme: Light**, **Theme: Dark**, or **Theme: Auto**.

With **Auto** (the default when you have never chosen), the admin follows your browser or operating system preference — switch your computer to dark mode and UnoPim follows. Once you pick Light or Dark, your choice is remembered.

## The left navigation

The sidebar is collapsed by default, showing only icons so your content gets the full width of the screen. Hover over an icon to fly out its submenu.

Prefer it open? Expand it with the **Collapse/Expand** control at the bottom of the sidebar. UnoPim remembers your choice for the next time you sign in.

## Pages load without reloading

Moving between admin pages no longer reloads the whole browser page. Clicking a menu item or saving a form swaps just the content, so navigation and saves feel instant.

You lose nothing you are used to: the address bar updates, the browser **Back** and **Forward** buttons work, and you can still bookmark or share any admin page.

## The Save / Discard bar

Edit forms no longer have a separate save button in every corner. Instead, one global bar handles saving:

- The bar appears at the top of the page only after you actually change something — it says **You have unsaved changes** and tells you how many sections or fields were modified.
- Changed fields are marked with an **Unsaved** badge, so you can find your own edits at a glance.
- **Save changes** saves everything at once.
- **Discard** restores every field to its original value. UnoPim asks you to confirm first.
- If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, UnoPim warns you before anything is lost.

<ImagePopup src="/assets/3.0/images/introduction/save-bar.png" alt="The global Save / Discard bar with unsaved sections" />

## Quick-create dialogs

You no longer leave your current page to create catalog structure. Attributes, attribute groups, attribute families, category fields, and association types can all be created from a small dialog:

1. Click the **Create** button on the listing.
2. Type a name in your catalog language.
3. UnoPim generates the code for you — you can edit it before saving.
4. Save. The record is created and you can open it for full editing whenever you like.

<ImagePopup src="/assets/3.0/images/introduction/quick-create-modal.png" alt="Quick-create dialog with an auto-generated code" />

## Toast notifications

Confirmations and errors now appear as small toast messages in the corner of the screen. They confirm a save, report a problem, and disappear on their own — no more messages pushing your page content around.

## Signing in and recovering access

The login page was redesigned and gained two practical additions:

- **Remember me** — tick it to stay signed in on your own computer.
- **Forgot password** — enter your email address to receive a reset link, and set a new password from that email. No administrator needed.

## Your personal catalog settings

Every user now has a personal **Catalog Locale** and **Default Channel**, separate from the interface language. They decide which language and channel UnoPim opens for you when you edit products or browse grids. Set them in **My Account**, or ask your administrator — see [Users](../settings/users.md).

## Profile pictures via Gravatar

If you have not uploaded a profile image, UnoPim can show the [Gravatar](https://gravatar.com) picture linked to your email address instead. Prefer not to? Untick **Use Gravatar image** in your account settings and UnoPim shows your initials.

::: tip
All of these preferences — theme, sidebar state, catalog locale, default channel — are per user. Your choices never affect your colleagues.
:::
